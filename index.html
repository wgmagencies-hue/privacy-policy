Privacy Policy for Smart Keyboard for LG TVs

Effective date: August 23, 2026
Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. What the app does

Smart Keyboard for LG TVs is an Android app that controls an LG webOS TV on your own Wi‑Fi network: virtual keyboard, remote-control buttons, touchpad, and — if you choose to turn it on — content monitoring that records which apps are being opened on the TV, plus parental rules that ask the app to leave those apps. Controlling the TV happens directly between your phone and your TV. Nothing has to be online for the keyboard or remote to work.

3. Where data is stored and who processes it

Server-side data (account, TV, viewing history, searches, rules) is stored in our Supabase project — a hosted PostgreSQL database with row-level security, so an account can only read the rows for TVs it owns or has been shared. Supabase is our only processor besides Google Play (Billing and purchase records).

4. How data travels

  • Phone ↔ our server: HTTPS/TLS.
  • Phone ↔ TV: your own local Wi‑Fi network. The app tries the TV’s encrypted endpoint first and falls back to a plain one when the TV only answers in plain text, in which case the commands and the pairing key cross your LAN unencrypted. Even on the encrypted endpoint the TV’s self-signed certificate is accepted without being checked against a certificate authority, so it keeps casual eavesdroppers out but not a determined attacker already inside your network. Either way this traffic stays inside your network — it does not pass through our servers. Use a network you control, and avoid open or shared Wi‑Fi.

On the phone, data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is not separately encrypted by the app beyond what your device does for its own storage.

5. Retention

  • Unsynced viewing sessions stay on the phone in a queue capped at 500 entries and searches at 1000; the queue is emptied when it is delivered, and the oldest entries are dropped if you stay offline for a long time.
  • Server-side history is kept until you delete it — removing a TV from the dashboard deletes the viewing history, searches and rules recorded against it — or until you delete your account, which removes every row the account owns.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, free of charge, from either place:

  • In the app: TV Content Monitoring → Delete Account & Erase All Data. This removes the account and everything it owns on the server and wipes the app’s local data on the phone.
  • By request: email wgmagencies@gmail.com and we will delete the account manually.

If a web dashboard is available for your account, the Delete account control in its sidebar does exactly the same thing as the in-app button. Deleting the account is irreversible. Signing out or “unlinking” a TV keeps the account and its history, so it is not the same thing.

8. Children

The app is aimed at an adult who manages a shared living-room TV, and its target age is 16 and older. It is not directed at children, we do not knowingly collect data from children, and the parental rules are intended to be operated by a parent or guardian on a TV they own.
