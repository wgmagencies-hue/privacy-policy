Privacy Policy for Smart Keyboard for LG TVs

Effective date: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Smart Keyboard for LG TVs ("the App") is an Android application that lets users control their LG WebOS TV with a virtual keyboard, remote-control buttons, and touchpad gestures. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information We Collect

CategoryWhat we collectWhy we collect it
Device & ConnectionTV device ID, IP address, Wi-Fi SSIDTo discover and maintain connection to the TV and send remote commands.
Usage & Session DataApp session timestamps, current app name, playback state, session durationProvide usage statistics on dashboard and enable trial tracking.
Billing / SubscriptionGoogle Play Billing info (product ID, purchase status)Manage PRO subscription and enforce 24-hour trial.
Crash DataStack traces (on crash only)To debug crashes and improve stability.

3. How We Use the Data

We use the data to maintain TV connection, show dashboard analytics, and manage subscriptions. No data is sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Local data is kept for 30 days or until cleared. You can delete all data anytime inside the app via Settings -> Clear All Data.

5. Security

All local data is stored in encrypted storage and network calls use secure HTTPS connections.
